# How work travels

**Routes through Corey James's agent gallery: how one signal passes from role to role until the work is done.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent gallery  

One signal can travel through several roles. Each route shows who picks the work up and who it is handed to.

## [A comment becomes a customer](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-comment-becomes-a-customer)

The Reputation Guard answers a question under a post. The Lead Catcher opens the deal, the Sales Engineer runs the demo, the Accounts Office collects and the Customer Success Manager sends the welcome.

1. [The Reputation Guard](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/reputation-guard/): For a brand manager or a small business owner.
2. [The Lead Catcher](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/lead-catcher/): For a sales team that loses inquiries.
3. [The Sales Engineer](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/sales-engineer/): For a sales team with more demos than engineers.
4. [The Accounts Office](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/accounts-office/): For a freelancer, a finance lead or anyone owed money.
5. [The Customer Success Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/customer-success-manager/): For a subscription business.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Company Records Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/company-records-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/company-records-manager/): It keeps one record per customer company, links the people and deals to it, and imports company lists.
- [Deal File Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk/): Files each document against the right deal, company or contact, finds it on request and removes outdated copies.
- [Lead Capture Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk/): When someone shows buying interest in a Facebook message, an X direct message, a website chat or an email, it creates the contact and company, opens a deal, sets a follow-up task and answers with a payment link where that fits.
- [Page Inbox Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector/): It collects new Page comments and messages, and on a slower cycle tagged posts and ratings, so nothing waits for someone to look.
- [Sales Pipeline Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager/): It keeps every deal in the right stage of the right pipeline, links deals to the people and companies involved, imports deals from other tools, and reports the pipeline.
- [Subscription Change Handler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler/): When a customer upgrades, downgrades or cancels it updates their record and loyalty status, and for a cancellation sends a retention message and records the reason.

## [A complaint becomes a release](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-complaint-becomes-a-release)

The Support Desk finds a defect and files it. The Ticket Clearer fixes it, the Change Board clears it, and the Training Department remakes the help video. The Support Desk tells the customer it shipped.

1. [The Support Desk](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/support-desk/): For a support lead, or a founder who answers customers alone.
2. [The Ticket Clearer](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/ticket-clearer/): For an engineering manager with a queue of small tickets.
3. [The Change Board](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/change-board/): For a manager about to approve a risky change.
4. [The Training Department](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/training-department/): For a support or training lead.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Help Article Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker/): Turns a help article into a short captioned how-to video so customers can watch the fix instead of reading it.
- [Issue Triage Engineer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer/): Reads each new issue, finds the code most likely at fault and comments the suspect file and line with a triage label so a developer starts in the right place.

## [A meeting becomes shipped software](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-meeting-becomes-shipped-software)

The Chief of Staff's minutes contain a feature request. The Software Department builds it, and the Presenter demonstrates it at the next meeting.

1. [The Chief of Staff](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/chief-of-staff/): For a founder with more meetings than hours.
2. [The Software Department](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/software-department/): For a founder with a large build and a small team.
3. [The Presenter](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/presenter/): For a founder with a recurring update to give.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Document Writer and Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor/): Drafts a plan, checklist or report in the team chat, revises it when asked, keeps every earlier version, and retires documents that are out of date.
- [Slide Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/slide-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/slide-presenter/): Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.

## [A decision becomes a launch](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-decision-becomes-a-launch)

The Advisory Board settles the plan. The Film Studio makes the film, the Launch Manager runs the week, the Shop Manager handles the orders and the Morning Brief reports the result.

1. [The Advisory Board](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/advisory-board/): For a leadership team facing a hard call.
2. [The Film Studio](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/film-studio/): For a marketing lead with a story and no crew.
3. [The Launch Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/launch-manager/): For a founder with a product shipping on Friday.
4. [The Shop Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/shop-manager/): For an online shop owner.
5. [The Morning Brief](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/morning-brief/): For an owner who wants one message a day.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Coupon Drop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager/): It creates a coupon collection for a promotion, loads the codes, sends them in an email or text campaign, and tracks the orders and revenue the coupon brought in.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.

## [An alert becomes a customer notice](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#an-alert-becomes-a-customer-notice)

The Night Shift contains the fault. The Reputation Guard sends the notice and answers the replies. The Record Keeper records what happened, and the Stand-up Host walks the team through it.

1. [The Night Shift](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/night-shift/): For a one-person operations team.
2. [The Reputation Guard](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/reputation-guard/): For a brand manager or a small business owner.
3. [The Record Keeper](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/record-keeper/): For whoever owns a system now.
4. [The Stand-up Host](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/stand-up-host/): For an engineering or operations team.

## [A failed payment becomes a kept account](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-failed-payment-becomes-a-kept-account)

The Revenue Rescuer recovers the payment. The Customer Success Manager checks in, and the Loyalty Director moves the customer up a tier.

1. [The Revenue Rescuer](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/revenue-rescuer/): For a finance lead who is tired of losing customers to expired cards.
2. [The Customer Success Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/customer-success-manager/): For a subscription business.
3. [The Loyalty Director](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/loyalty-director/): For a retail marketing lead.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Customer Lifecycle Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent/): It welcomes new buyers, follows their engagement, recovers failed payments, and works to keep subscribers who move to cancel.
- [Loyalty Member Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge/): It enrols customers, places them in the right tier, and answers any member's question about their points, tier or history.
- [Subscription Retention Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent/): It moves the customer to the right list, sends a retention or farewell message, and flags valuable cancellations for a person to call.
- [Win-back Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/win-back-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/win-back-agent/): It finds customers who have gone quiet, gives them a coupon, sends a win-back email or text, and reports who came back and what they spent.

## [An acquisition becomes a working team](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#an-acquisition-becomes-a-working-team)

The Due-Diligence Analyst reads the codebase. The Second Opinion audits it, the Company Librarian files the documents and the Onboarding Buddy brings each engineer up to speed.

1. [The Due-Diligence Analyst](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/due-diligence-analyst/): For a CTO inheriting or acquiring a codebase.
2. [The Second Opinion](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/second-opinion/): For a CTO who wants a critical system read by someone who did not build it.
3. [The Company Librarian](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/company-librarian/): For a team whose knowledge is scattered.
4. [The Onboarding Buddy](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/onboarding-buddy/): For a manager onboarding someone.

Specialists these roles share:

- [Dead Code Finder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/dead-code-finder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/dead-code-finder/): Lists functions and types that nothing appears to call, as candidates for a person to review.

- [New Hire Guide](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/new-hire-guide) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/new-hire-guide/): Walks a new team member through the team's shared files and recent decisions, by voice or chat, and leaves them an orientation document to come back to.

## [A new model becomes a story](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-new-model-becomes-a-story)

The Model Shrinker compresses it, the Model Launch Manager rolls it out under the Spend Controller's limit, and the Newsroom writes about it from the engineering record.

1. [The Model Shrinker](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/model-shrinker/): For an AI team that needs a large model on a smaller card.
2. [The Model Launch Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/model-launch-manager/): For a platform lead shipping a new model version.
3. [The Spend Controller](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/spend-controller/): For whoever signs off on compute spend.
4. [The Newsroom](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/newsroom/): For a brand with something to say every day and nobody to say it.

Specialists these roles share:

- [AI Answer Accuracy Checker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/ai-answer-accuracy-checker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/ai-answer-accuracy-checker/): This agent checks whether a change alters the model's answers, finds where any difference starts and gives a clear yes or no.
- [Model Rollout Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/model-rollout-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/model-rollout-manager/): Moves a new model version into service: builds it, sends a harmless test job through, benchmarks it against the current version, brings up new workers, drains the old ones, and puts the old version back if the new one is worse.
- [Release Readiness Reviewer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/release-readiness-reviewer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/release-readiness-reviewer/): This agent pulls together the regression results, accuracy checks, load tests and package review for a new version and writes one go or no-go summary.

3. [The AI Workforce Manager](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/ai-workforce-manager/): For a manager running a team of AI agents.

1. [The Migration Planner](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/migration-planner/): For an engineering lead who just read a vendor's deprecation notice.
2. [The Planning Room](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/planning-room/): For a founder or manager deciding what to build next.
3. [The Software Department](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/software-department/): For a founder with a large build and a small team.
4. [The Change Board](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/change-board/): For a manager about to approve a risky change.
