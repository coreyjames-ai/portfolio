# Agents that staff a company

**Corey James's agent gallery: specialist agents that each do one job, combined into whole roles that staff a company.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent gallery  

I built a foundation of specialist agents that each do one job. Combined, the same specialists staff whole roles, such as a chief of staff or a night shift. A new role is assembled from specialists that already exist.

## How work travels

One signal can travel through several roles. Each route shows who picks the work up and who it is handed to.

## Seven kinds of agent

Some agents do the work. Others supervise them, challenge their decisions, keep what they learn, run their machines, audit them and speak for them.

- [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work): Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.
- [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people): Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.
- [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents): Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.
- [Challenges decisions](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=challenges-decisions): Reviews a plan or a result and argues with it before it ships.
- [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge): Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.
- [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure): Keeps machines, queues and data moving.
- [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards): Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.
