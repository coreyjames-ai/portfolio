# Pricing Change Watchdog

## What it does

It compares the change with the approved offer and alerts the owner when a price or link has drifted.

For a finance lead or a product marketing manager.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Store, loyalty and payments.

Wakes on: A product, price or payment link is created or edited.
