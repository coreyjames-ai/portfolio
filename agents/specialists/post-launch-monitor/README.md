## What it does

It confirms where the post went live, re-posts to any platform that failed, and gives the owner the live links.

For a content producer or a marketing lead.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Social media.

Wakes on: A post goes live, half-publishes, fails on one platform, or is deleted.
