# Revenue Reporter

**It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent specialist  

## What it does

It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes. It sends a weekly summary to the team's chat.

For a founder or finance lead.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Store, loyalty and payments.
