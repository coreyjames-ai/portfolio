# AI Answer Accuracy Checker

## What it does

This agent checks whether a change alters the model's answers, finds where any difference starts and gives a clear yes or no.

For an AI platform lead who must not let a speed-up change the answers.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: AI models and performance.
