## What it does

Splits a job into child jobs, hands them to short-lived workers, tracks each one and collects the results.

For a team lead with work that splits into many small jobs.

Kind: [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure). Keeps machines, queues and data moving.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
