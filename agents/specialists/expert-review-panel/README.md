## What it does

Puts a question to several AI models at once, has them propose, challenge and revise in turns, and returns a written record of where they agree, where they differ, and what needs your decision.

For an engineering manager or strategy lead.

Kind: [Challenges decisions](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=challenges-decisions). Reviews a plan or a result and argues with it before it ships.

Group: Decisions and deliberation.
