## What it does

It convenes several AI models as a panel on your question and agrees the goal with you first. The models then work in parallel, challenge each other and propose numbered versions, and the panel closes only when all of them agree on exactly one version.

For a founder or leadership team facing a hard call.

Kind: [Challenges decisions](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=challenges-decisions). Reviews a plan or a result and argues with it before it ships.

Group: Decisions and deliberation.
