## What it does

From one brief it sends the launch email, the launch text message and WhatsApp template, posts on X and the Facebook Page, cross-posts to other channels, pins the announcement and opens a payment link. It then reports the response across all of them.

For a founder or marketing lead with a product launch.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Email, text and messaging.
