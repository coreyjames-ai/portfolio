## What it does

Takes a very large pile of prompts, packs them so every GPU stays busy, collects the answers after the submitting programs have gone home, and writes the results back with every item accounted for. You get a finished dataset rather than a babysitting job.

For an AI or ML team lead with millions of prompts to process.

Kind: [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure). Keeps machines, queues and data moving.

Group: AI models and performance.
