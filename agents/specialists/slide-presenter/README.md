# Slide Presenter

**Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.**

## What it does

Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.

For a sales team or a founder giving a recurring update.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
