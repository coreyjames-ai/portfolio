# Agent Team Designer

**Takes a project's goal and its source material, explores that material through the search tools, proposes a roster of specialist agents, and writes their skill guides and agent definitions.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/agent-team-designer/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent specialist  

## What it does

Takes a project's goal and its source material, explores that material through the search tools, proposes a roster of specialist agents, and writes their skill guides and agent definitions. A person reviews them first; they are then deployed, archived and restored on request, and the team can be stood up with those members.

For a founder or team lead who wants a set of specialist assistants built around their own material.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
