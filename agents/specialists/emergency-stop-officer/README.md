## What it does

Stops one agent or every agent in a given role at once, records what was running and confirms nothing is left.

For an AI operations lead who needs everything of one kind stopped now.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
