# AI Session Supervisor

**Keeps interactive assistant sessions running on the right machine, delivers team messages to them, and accounts for every transcript and every unit of usage, without giving a session access to the database.**

## What it does

Keeps interactive assistant sessions running on the right machine, delivers team messages to them, and accounts for every transcript and every unit of usage, without giving a session access to the database.

For a team lead running AI coding assistants on shared machines.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
