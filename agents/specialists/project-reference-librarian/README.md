## What it does

It answers questions from the reference documents that belong to one project, with page numbers, and ignores documents from other projects.

For a team sitting on a pile of specifications and manuals.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
