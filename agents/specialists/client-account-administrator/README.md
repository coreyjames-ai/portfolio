## What it does

It opens and closes a sub-account for each client or brand, sets each one's plan and features, groups them, and hands the right people access with single sign-on links. It takes the account paperwork off the owner's desk and keeps every brand separated.

For an agency owner or a company running several brands.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Email, text and messaging.
