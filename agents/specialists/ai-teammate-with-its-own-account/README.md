## What it does

Joins the company directory as a named colleague with its own email, chat and calendar, so work it does is signed with its name and the company's normal access rules decide what it may touch.

For a founder or operations lead.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
