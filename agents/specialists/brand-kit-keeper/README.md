# Brand Kit Keeper

**Reads the company website and proposes a brand kit of logo, colours, fonts and social links.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-kit-keeper/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent specialist  

## What it does

Reads the company website and proposes a brand kit of logo, colours, fonts and social links. Once approved, it keeps the kit current so every template uses it.

For a founder who wants every email and page to look like the same company.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.

Group: Content, film and brand.
