## What it does

It keeps one record per customer company, links the people and deals to it, and imports company lists.

For a sales or account management team.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.

Group: Sales, customers and service.
