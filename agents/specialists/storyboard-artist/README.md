# Storyboard Artist

**Turns a story into a shot-by-shot plan with timing, camera moves, the few lines of on-screen text and how each shot will be made.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist/

## What it does

Turns a story into a shot-by-shot plan with timing, camera moves, the few lines of on-screen text and how each shot will be made. It folds every round of notes back into the same plan.

For a creative director or content producer.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Content, film and brand.
