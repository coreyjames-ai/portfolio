## What it does

It opens a case, hands it to the right specialist, writes back to the customer by email, and closes the case with a note of what was done.

For a support desk lead or a founder answering customers alone.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Sales, customers and service.

Wakes on: A customer submits a help request on the website or in the app.
