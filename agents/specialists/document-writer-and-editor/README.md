## What it does

Drafts a plan, checklist or report in the team chat, revises it when asked, keeps every earlier version, and retires documents that are out of date.

For anyone who owns plans, checklists or reports.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
