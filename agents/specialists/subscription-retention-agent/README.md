## What it does

It moves the customer to the right list, sends a retention or farewell message, and flags valuable cancellations for a person to call.

For a subscription business owner or a customer success lead.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Store, loyalty and payments.

Wakes on: A subscription changes plan or is cancelled.
