# Engineering Cost Analyst

## What it does

Attributes every model call to the task, role and phase that spent it and turns the totals into cost and human-equivalent effort hours per project.

For a finance lead or CTO paying for AI engineering.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
