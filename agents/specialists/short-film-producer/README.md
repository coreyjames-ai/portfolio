## What it does

Takes an approved story and returns a finished short film with sound, plus a version sized for phones. It runs the whole production, from shot plan to final mix, and comes back with the film and a plain note on its weak spots.

For a film or content producer.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Content, film and brand.
