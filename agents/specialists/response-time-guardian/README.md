## What it does

It brings the overdue item to the top, answers it or escalates it to a person, and reports response times across platforms.

For a customer service lead or a social media manager.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Social media.

Wakes on: Any social message left unanswered for more than an hour.
