## What it does

It builds each campaign from a template for a chosen list or segment, sends a test, schedules or sends it, and files it in the right folder. Afterwards it sends the report and exports who opened, clicked or bounced.

For a marketing lead.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Email, text and messaging.
