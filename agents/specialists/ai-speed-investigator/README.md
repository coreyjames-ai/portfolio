## What it does

This agent finds where the time goes in each AI response, from the full request down to waiting, copying and the slowest steps, and compares serving modes side by side.

For an AI platform lead whose responses are slower than expected.

Kind: [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure). Keeps machines, queues and data moving.
