## What it does

It welcomes new buyers, follows their engagement, recovers failed payments, and works to keep subscribers who move to cancel.

For a founder or a customer success lead.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Signal to done.

Wakes on: A purchase, then each later customer signal.
