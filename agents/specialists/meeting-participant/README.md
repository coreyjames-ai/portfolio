## What it does

It listens, answers aloud, takes on tasks, raises its hand when a result is ready, and shows its work on screen or in a presentation.

For a team lead or an executive who wants an AI colleague in the meeting.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.

Wakes on: Someone speaks to the agent by name in a Microsoft Teams meeting or call.
