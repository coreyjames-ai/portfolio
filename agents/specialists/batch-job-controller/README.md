# Batch Job Controller

**Runs a large job on a local card or a rented one, pauses, scales or stops it mid-flight, turns a one-off job into an always-on service, and isolates a job that misbehaves.**

## What it does

Runs a large job on a local card or a rented one, pauses, scales or stops it mid-flight, turns a one-off job into an always-on service, and isolates a job that misbehaves. Every control action is kept on record for review.

For a data engineering lead running long jobs.

Kind: [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure). Keeps machines, queues and data moving.

Group: Machines, data and operations.
