## What it does

Puts several models from different vendors in one structured debate that works through numbered plan revisions and ends only when all agree, then hands over a plan ready to execute.

For a leadership team weighing a hard plan.

Kind: [Challenges decisions](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=challenges-decisions). Reviews a plan or a result and argues with it before it ships.

Group: Decisions and deliberation.
