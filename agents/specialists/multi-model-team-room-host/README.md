## What it does

It brings AI models from different vendors into one room as named teammates with their own accounts, makes them take turns so they never talk over each other or set each other off, and adds a new model family by a repeatable sign-up routine.

For an engineering manager who wants several AI models on the team.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
