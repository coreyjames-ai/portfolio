## What it does

After the meeting it takes the transcript, has a panel reconcile what was decided and what is still open, records each decision in the project's memory, and posts the minutes as a document.

For a leadership team that meets by video.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
