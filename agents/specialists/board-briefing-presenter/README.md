## What it does

It turns an agreed plan into a briefing deck, joins the video meeting as a named digital colleague, presents the deck slide by slide, and drops the plan link into the meeting chat.

For a founder who reports to a board or an investor group.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.
