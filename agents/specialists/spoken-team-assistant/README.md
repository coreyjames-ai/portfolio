# Spoken Team Assistant

## What it does

It answers a spoken question with a short reply out loud and posts the full written answer in the right chat room. It can move between rooms, address a teammate, start a panel discussion or hand heavy work to a stronger model.

For a manager who would rather talk than type.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
