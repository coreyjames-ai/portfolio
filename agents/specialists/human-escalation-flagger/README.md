# Human Escalation Flagger

**Marks a ticket as needing a person when the AI reviewers cannot settle it, with a summary of the disagreement.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/human-escalation-flagger/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent specialist  

## What it does

Marks a ticket as needing a person when the AI reviewers cannot settle it, with a summary of the disagreement.

For a team that lets AI workers handle tickets and wants a person on the hard ones.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Building software.

## Roles it serves

- [The Delegate](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/delegate) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/delegate/): For a founder who cannot be in every decision.
- [The Review Panel](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/review-panel) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/review-panel/): For a tech lead who wants every change read by more than one specialist.
