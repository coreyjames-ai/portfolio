## What it does

It takes the request, runs the work on a worker while the meeting continues, posts the result in the meeting chat, and records the item in the minutes.

For a team lead or an executive.

Kind: [Does the work](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=does-the-work). Writes, builds, renders and ships the thing that was asked for.

Group: Signal to done.

Wakes on: A request spoken during a Microsoft Teams meeting.
