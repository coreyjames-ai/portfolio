## What it does

Keeps the records the business defines for itself, such as vehicles, policies or properties, links each to its customer, and updates or removes them in bulk.

For an operations lead whose business tracks something a standard customer record has no place for.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.
