# Team AI Colleague

## What it does

It is a named AI colleague in the team chat that answers when someone addresses it, in the right thread, already knowing the project's recent history and files. It keeps its own memory even when the model behind it changes.

For a team lead whose team works in a group chat.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Meetings, voice and the AI colleague.
