## What it does

Takes the minutes after every meeting from the recording and transcript, and sends everyone the same version, updating it when someone points out a correction.

For a team lead who runs weekly meetings.

Kind: [Keeps knowledge](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=keeps-knowledge). Records what happened and finds it again when someone asks.
