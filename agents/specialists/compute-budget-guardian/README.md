# Compute Budget Guardian

## What it does

Holds the line on the daily spend that only you set: it refuses any start the budget cannot cover and says why, warns you as spend climbs, and stops paid machines at the cap. No agent can raise the number; only you can, at your own terminal.

For a founder or finance lead who signs off on compute spend.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Machines, data and operations.
