## What it does

Starts agents from role definitions in locked-down containers, pauses, resumes or stops them, and reloads the role and tool catalogs when they change.

For an IT operations lead who starts and stops agents on demand.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
