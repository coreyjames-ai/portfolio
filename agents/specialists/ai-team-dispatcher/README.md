# AI Team Dispatcher

**Starts an AI agent for each role you need, each with its own limited access, workspace and budget, on the machine you choose.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/ai-team-dispatcher/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent specialist  

## What it does

Starts an AI agent for each role you need, each with its own limited access, workspace and budget, on the machine you choose. It pauses, stops or replaces agents, stops every agent in a role at once, and reloads the role list when you add a new one.

For an engineering manager running a team of AI agents.

Kind: [Supervises other agents](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=supervises-other-agents). Staffs a team, starts and stops its members and reports on them.

Group: Managing an AI workforce.
