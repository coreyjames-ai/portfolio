# AI Provider Switchboard

**Sends every agent's model calls to an approved provider, keeps each provider inside its rate limit, steps down to a cheaper tier when a quota runs out, and blocks traffic that a provider's terms do not allow.**

## What it does

Sends every agent's model calls to an approved provider, keeps each provider inside its rate limit, steps down to a cheaper tier when a quota runs out, and blocks traffic that a provider's terms do not allow.

For an AI team lead paying for several model providers.

Kind: [Runs infrastructure](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=runs-infrastructure). Keeps machines, queues and data moving.

Group: AI models and performance.
