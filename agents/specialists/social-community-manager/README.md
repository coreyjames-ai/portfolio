## What it does

It reads the thread, replies in the brand voice, and records each reply with the post it belongs to.

For a marketing lead or a social media manager.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Social media.

Wakes on: A comment or mention on a post the business published on any connected platform.
