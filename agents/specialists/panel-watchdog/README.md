## What it does

It watches a panel for spending limits, interrupted turns and idle sessions, pauses instead of forcing a rushed answer, recovers interrupted work in place, and tells you by voice what state the panel is in.

For anyone running long AI panel discussions.

Kind: [Audits and guards](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=audits-and-guards). Checks access, spend and behaviour, and raises the alarm.

Group: Decisions and deliberation.
