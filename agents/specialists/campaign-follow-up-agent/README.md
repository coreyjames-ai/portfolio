## What it does

It moves engaged readers into a follow-up sequence and hands the most engaged ones to sales.

For a marketing lead or a sales team.

Kind: [Speaks to people](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/?kind=speaks-to-people). Answers in chat, on calls and by message, in plain language.

Group: Email, text and messaging.

Wakes on: A reader opens a campaign or clicks a link in it.
