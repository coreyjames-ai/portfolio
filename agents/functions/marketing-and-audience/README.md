# Marketing and audience

**Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent function  

## The roles

- [The Newsroom](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/newsroom) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/newsroom/): For a brand with something to say every day and nobody to say it.
- [The Launch Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/launch-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/launch-manager/): For a founder with a product shipping on Friday.
- [The Conversation Finder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/conversation-finder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/conversation-finder/): For a founder building an audience.
- [The Reputation Guard](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/reputation-guard) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/reputation-guard/): For a brand manager or a small business owner.
- [The Film Studio](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/film-studio) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/film-studio/): For a marketing lead with a story and no crew.
- [The Training Department](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/training-department) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/training-department/): For a support or training lead.
- [The Email Marketer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/email-marketer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/email-marketer/): For a marketing lead who owns the list.
- [The Agency Back Office](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/agency-back-office) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/agency-back-office/): For an agency or a company with several brands.
- [The Social Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/social-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/social-desk/): For a social media manager with a full calendar and no spare hands.
- [The Rapid Responder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/rapid-responder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/rapid-responder/): For a founder who has a take and ten minutes.
- [The Ad Buyer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/ad-buyer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/ad-buyer/): For a producer with a finished video and a small budget.
- [The Recruiting Marketer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/recruiting-marketer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/recruiting-marketer/): For a hiring manager with an open role.
- [The Developer Advocate](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/developer-advocate) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/developer-advocate/): For an engineering-led company whose product is hard to explain.
- [The Newsletter Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/newsletter-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/newsletter-editor/): For a team that owes its subscribers something every week.
- [The Message Centre](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/message-centre) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/message-centre/): For a customer team whose customers live on their phones.

## Specialists these roles share

- [Cross-Platform Publisher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher/): It checks a post against each platform's rules, uploads its media, publishes it now or cross-posts it, and withdraws it if needed.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.
- [Brand Image Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-image-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-image-maker/): Turns an image brief into finished images in the brand's look, with the words set by a designer template instead of drawn by the image model.
- [Brand Kit Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-kit-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-kit-keeper/): Reads the company website and proposes a brand kit of logo, colours, fonts and social links.
- [Caption and Format Specialist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist/): Makes every version a channel needs from one master: vertical, square-ish and wide sizes, web and audio formats, captioned and silent copies.
- [Carousel and Cover Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer/): Builds carousels, cover cards, hook cards and thumbnails from fixed brand templates, so every slide carries the same type, colours and closing logo.
- [Consent and Opt-in Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer/): It signs new subscribers up through a confirm-by-email step, records where consent came from, honours every unsubscribe and block, and keeps an export for anyone who asks what is held.
- [Contact Data Steward](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/contact-data-steward) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/contact-data-steward/): It keeps every contact record complete and current: it creates and updates contacts, adds the fields the business needs, and runs bulk imports and exports when data moves.
- [Content Calendar Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer/): Works from the posting calendar so every upcoming slot has an approved, checked image or video, starts production when one is missing and schedules the post on each network.
- [Content-to-Conversation Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/content-to-conversation-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/content-to-conversation-producer/): It finds the live conversation on the topic, drafts the post, generates the image, publishes across platforms, answers every comment the post attracts, and reports how it performed.
- [Deliverability Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deliverability-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deliverability-keeper/): It watches bounces, spam complaints and blocked addresses, removes bad addresses from lists, clears hard bounces once fixed, and blocks or unblocks whole domains.
- [Email Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-campaign-manager/): It builds each campaign from a template for a chosen list or segment, sends a test, schedules or sends it, and files it in the right folder.
- [Email List Hygiene Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-list-hygiene-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-list-hygiene-agent/): It removes the address from active lists, records why, and keeps a running view of sender health for the team.
- [Email Template Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-template-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-template-designer/): It keeps the library of campaign and transactional templates, previews them with real data, pulls live content from external feeds, and sends test copies before anyone uses a template.
- [Engineering Story Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/engineering-story-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/engineering-story-writer/): Picks a real part of the product, traces how it works, writes a plain-language explainer post from that trace, and schedules it on social platforms after the voice edit.
- [Explainer Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/explainer-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/explainer-video-maker/): Turns a written page or article into a short narrated explainer with designed cards, captions and moving backgrounds, ready to post with the link in the text.
- [Facebook Page Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager/): Uploads photos and videos to the business page, publishes or schedules the post, answers comments and reports how the post did.
- [Import and Export Tracker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/import-and-export-tracker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/import-and-export-tracker/): Follows every background import and export, says when each one finished or failed, and hands back the result file.
- [Launch Campaign Runner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner/): From one brief it sends the launch email, the launch text message and WhatsApp template, posts on X and the Facebook Page, cross-posts to other channels, pins the announcement and opens a payment link.
- [List and Segment Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/list-and-segment-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/list-and-segment-keeper/): It keeps mailing lists and segments tidy, adds and removes people, organises lists into folders, and reports who is on each list.
- [Media Drop-off Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/media-drop-off-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/media-drop-off-desk/): Sends the owner a private upload page, waits for the photos or video to arrive, checks them against each platform's limits and attaches them to the waiting post.
- [On-Screen Content Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/on-screen-content-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/on-screen-content-designer/): Builds realistic but invented screens, terminals and dashboards for videos so no real log, customer name or address ever appears on camera.
- [Page Inbox Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector/): It collects new Page comments and messages, and on a slower cycle tagged posts and ratings, so nothing waits for someone to look.
- [Paid Social Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/paid-social-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/paid-social-campaign-manager/): It sets up and adjusts paid social campaigns through the ads manager, reads delivery, completion and cost per click, pauses the weaker ad and removes finished campaigns.
- [Post Launch Monitor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor/): It confirms where the post went live, re-posts to any platform that failed, and gives the owner the live links.
- [Product Demo Clip Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/product-demo-clip-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/product-demo-clip-maker/): Records a terminal or product session, masks addresses and private details, paces it and returns a captioned demo clip ready to post.
- [Reddit Community Participant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/reddit-community-participant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/reddit-community-participant/): It reads the threads where the business's subject is discussed, drafts posts for the owner to submit, and adds, corrects or removes its own comments from the owner's signed-in account.
- [Release Notes Mailer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/release-notes-mailer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/release-notes-mailer/): Summarises what changed over a period from the record of completed work and the code it touched, and sends the notes to a subscriber list as an email campaign.
- [Render-to-Release Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/render-to-release-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/render-to-release-agent/): It confirms the finished clip, delivers the file, publishes it with its post copy across platforms, and watches the first reactions.
- [Revenue Attribution Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-attribution-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-attribution-analyst/): It shows which email campaigns, text campaigns and automated journeys brought in orders and revenue, and which products sold through them.
- [Sending Domain and Sender Steward](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward/): It adds the business's sending domain, gives back the DNS records to publish, checks authentication, and creates and confirms each sender address so mail arrives as the business.
- [Short Film Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/short-film-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/short-film-producer/): Takes an approved story and returns a finished short film with sound, plus a version sized for phones.
- [Social Account Health Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer/): It checks every connected account's sign-in, permissions and usage limits and warns before a credential or allowance runs out.
- [Social Analytics Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter/): It reports reach and engagement per post and per day, follower growth and tracking-link results across every connected account, X and the Facebook Page.
- [Social Calendar Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager/): It keeps each brand's posting calendar: brand profiles, which accounts belong to which brand, the weekly posting slots, and every draft and scheduled post, including bulk uploads.
- [Social Community Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-community-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-community-manager/): It reads the thread, replies in the brand voice, and records each reply with the post it belongs to.
- [Social Inbox Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-inbox-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-inbox-manager/): It brings comments and mentions from all connected accounts into one inbox and answers each on the platform where it arrived.
- [Social Video Cutdown Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-video-cutdown-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-video-cutdown-editor/): Turns one finished video into feed versions: a cold open, a size-capped upload, a looping GIF and a carousel built from its best frames.
- [Storyboard Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist/): Turns a story into a shot-by-shot plan with timing, camera moves, the few lines of on-screen text and how each shot will be made.
- [Text Message Dispatcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher/): It sends one-off text messages such as delivery and appointment notices from templates, and reports what was delivered.
- [Trend Response Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/trend-response-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/trend-response-writer/): When a conversation takes off it writes a short post or reply that adds a fact from the business's own record, has a person approve it and publishes it.
- [Video Mix and Finishing Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor/): Assembles the shots into the cut, adds grain and grade, mixes effects and room tone to a broadcast loudness and checks that no second of the film is silent.
- [X Community Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-community-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-community-manager/): It watches each new post's replies in its first hour, answers questions with facts, likes good replies, quotes worthwhile posts, follows people it talks to, and closes each conversation.
- [X Conversation Scout](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-conversation-scout) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-conversation-scout/): It finds the live conversations worth joining, the accounts like the brand's best followers, and the hashtags and phrases actually being used, and keeps a history of what it has seen.
- [X Engagement Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-engagement-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-engagement-agent/): It reads each item and the conversation around it, replies, likes or quotes where that adds something, and marks each item handled so nothing is answered twice.
- [X Publisher and Reply Watcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-publisher-and-reply-watcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-publisher-and-reply-watcher/): Posts the chosen video or image to X, keeps an eye on replies and mentions, and answers questions that need a new fact with a fresh image.
- [X Reply Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-reply-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-reply-campaign-manager/): It finds threads to join, drafts reply options for a person to pick on their phone, posts the approved replies at a safe pace, follows each author and checks every reply landed correctly.
