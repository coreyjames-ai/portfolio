# Sales and customers

**Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent function  

## The roles

- [The Lead Catcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/lead-catcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/lead-catcher/): For a sales team that loses inquiries.
- [The Sales Engineer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/sales-engineer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/sales-engineer/): For a sales team with more demos than engineers.
- [The Customer Success Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/customer-success-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/customer-success-manager/): For a subscription business.
- [The Revenue Rescuer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/revenue-rescuer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/revenue-rescuer/): For a finance lead who is tired of losing customers to expired cards.
- [The Shop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/shop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/shop-manager/): For an online shop owner.
- [The Loyalty Director](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/loyalty-director) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/loyalty-director/): For a retail marketing lead.
- [The Accounts Office](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/accounts-office) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/accounts-office/): For a freelancer, a finance lead or anyone owed money.
- [The Support Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/support-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/support-desk/): For a support lead, or a founder who answers customers alone.
- [The Front Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/front-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/front-desk/): For a small business with a phone that rings.

## Specialists these roles share

- [Abandoned Checkout Follow-up](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up/): It finds checkouts that were started but never paid, looks up the customer, and sends one reminder with a fresh payment link.
- [Billing Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/billing-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/billing-desk/): It answers customers' billing questions by finding the payment, invoice or subscription, and makes approved changes such as updating a subscription.
- [Company Records Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/company-records-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/company-records-manager/): It keeps one record per customer company, links the people and deals to it, and imports company lists.
- [Coupon Drop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager/): It creates a coupon collection for a promotion, loads the codes, sends them in an email or text campaign, and tracks the orders and revenue the coupon brought in.
- [Customer Lifecycle Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent/): It welcomes new buyers, follows their engagement, recovers failed payments, and works to keep subscribers who move to cancel.
- [Customer Support Desk Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent/): It opens a case, hands it to the right specialist, writes back to the customer by email, and closes the case with a note of what was done.
- [Deal File Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk/): Files each document against the right deal, company or contact, finds it on request and removes outdated copies.
- [Invoice Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser/): When a payment fails or an invoice goes unpaid it tells the customer with a fresh payment link, reminds them once, and opens a follow-up task for a person.
- [Issue Triage Engineer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer/): Reads each new issue, finds the code most likely at fault and comments the suspect file and line with a triage label so a developer starts in the right place.
- [Loyalty Member Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge/): It enrols customers, places them in the right tier, and answers any member's question about their points, tier or history.
- [Payment Recovery Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent/): It sends the buyer a fresh payment link with a short note, watches for the payment, and tells the team whether the sale was recovered.
- [Payment Request Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector/): It sends a client a payment request with a reference and amount, turns on automatic reminders, checks whether it was paid, and withdraws requests that are no longer due.
- [Phone and Call Receptionist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist/): Answers direct calls to the AI teammate, listens, replies in a natural voice, and stops talking the moment the caller interrupts.
- [Points and Redemption Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk/): It credits points for purchases, takes them back on cancellations, and completes reward redemptions so balances are always right.
- [Pricing Change Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog/): It compares the change with the approved offer and alerts the owner when a price or link has drifted.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Sales Follow-up Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant/): It makes sure every conversation has a next step: it creates follow-up tasks with due dates, writes notes on deals, contacts and companies, and keeps the related files.
- [Sales Pipeline Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager/): It keeps every deal in the right stage of the right pipeline, links deals to the people and companies involved, imports deals from other tools, and reports the pipeline.
- [Subscription Change Handler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler/): When a customer upgrades, downgrades or cancels it updates their record and loyalty status, and for a cancellation sends a retention message and records the reason.
- [Subscription Retention Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent/): It moves the customer to the right list, sends a retention or farewell message, and flags valuable cancellations for a person to call.
- [Support Inbox Triage Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent/): It reads new support emails, chat messages and social messages, finds the answer in the business's knowledge base, drafts the reply for a person to approve, sends it and notes the case on the customer's record.
- [Team Knowledge Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk/): Answers questions in team chat from everything that team is allowed to see: the channel's own conversation, files shared there, the codebase, reference documents, libraries, past engineering notes and agent activity.
- [Voucher and Reward Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk/): It issues vouchers to members, redeems or revokes them, checks that a reward is valid, and reports how many codes are left.
- [Website Chat Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent/): It answers visitors in the website chat as a named agent, corrects or removes its own messages, and sends proactive messages such as order updates or announcements to one visitor or a batch.
- [Win-back Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/win-back-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/win-back-agent/): It finds customers who have gone quiet, gives them a coupon, sends a win-back email or text, and reports who came back and what they spent.
