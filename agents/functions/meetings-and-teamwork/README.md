- [The Company Librarian](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/company-librarian) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/company-librarian/): For a team whose knowledge is scattered.

## Specialists these roles share

- [Meeting Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator/): Keeps a large call orderly: it notices raised hands, calls on people by name in the order they asked, and keeps track of who is speaking and who has dropped off.
- [New Hire Guide](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/new-hire-guide) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/new-hire-guide/): Walks a new team member through the team's shared files and recent decisions, by voice or chat, and leaves them an orientation document to come back to.
