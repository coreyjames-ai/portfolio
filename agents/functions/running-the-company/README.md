# Running the company

**The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent function  

## The roles

- [The Chief of Staff](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/chief-of-staff) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/chief-of-staff/): For a founder with more meetings than hours.
- [The Pocket Operator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/pocket-operator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/pocket-operator/): For an owner who runs the business from a phone.
- [The Advisory Board](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/advisory-board) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/advisory-board/): For a leadership team facing a hard call.
- [The Morning Brief](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/morning-brief) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/morning-brief/): For an owner who wants one message a day.
- [The Digital Employee](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/digital-employee) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/digital-employee/): For a company that wants an AI colleague everyone can work with.
- [The Delegate](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/delegate) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/delegate/): For a founder who cannot be in every decision.
- [The Planning Room](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/planning-room) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/planning-room/): For a founder or manager deciding what to build next.
- [The Meeting Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/meeting-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/meeting-concierge/): For a team lead whose meetings end with "someone should look into that".
- [The Project Room](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/project-room) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/project-room/): For a team that runs its projects in group chat.
- [The Week in Review](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/week-in-review) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/week-in-review/): For a founder who wants Friday's numbers without building the report.
- [The Operations Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/roles/operations-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/operations-desk/): For a founder who needs a function staffed around the clock, such as support.

## Specialists these roles share

- [Decision Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-keeper/): Writes down every decision, constraint and open question as it is made, so nobody relitigates last month's call and new people can find the reasoning.
- [AI Teammate With Its Own Account](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/ai-teammate-with-its-own-account) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/ai-teammate-with-its-own-account/): Joins the company directory as a named colleague with its own email, chat and calendar, so work it does is signed with its name and the company's normal access rules decide what it may touch.
- [Action-Item Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser/): Pulls every promise made in a meeting into a list with owners and due dates, then nudges each owner by chat until the item is done.
- [Ask the Owner by Email Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/ask-the-owner-by-email-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/ask-the-owner-by-email-agent/): It emails the owner the question with options, waits for the reply at the team's own address, and continues the work with the answer.
- [Audio Briefing Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer/): Turns the week's project activity into a short narrated audio briefing that people can listen to on the move.
- [Background Task Worker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/background-task-worker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/background-task-worker/): Takes a task from chat or voice, does the file or code work in its own isolated workspace, reports back, picks the same thread up later, and shuts itself down when idle so it costs nothing.
- [Campaign Results Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst/): It reads how each email went, which version of an A/B test won, what each campaign earned in orders, and gives a plain recommendation for the next send.
- [Conversation Archivist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/conversation-archivist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/conversation-archivist/): It saves every message, file and link into the right project's memory as it is posted, records decisions and open questions so they can be found later, and briefs anyone who joins late.
- [Decision Announcer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-announcer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-announcer/): It takes a decision the panel agreed, drafts a short public post from the decision document, and puts it into the social posting queue for approval.
- [Decision Panel Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator/): It convenes several AI models as a panel on your question and agrees the goal with you first.
- [Document Writer and Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor/): Drafts a plan, checklist or report in the team chat, revises it when asked, keeps every earlier version, and retires documents that are out of date.
- [Expert Review Panel](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/expert-review-panel) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/expert-review-panel/): Puts a question to several AI models at once, has them propose, challenge and revise in turns, and returns a written record of where they agree, where they differ, and what needs your decision.
- [Idea-to-Approved-Plan Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/idea-to-approved-plan-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/idea-to-approved-plan-agent/): It runs a planning debate across several AI models, asks the owner to settle the open questions, and after the owner approves, starts a team on the plan.
- [Meeting Minute-Taker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-minute-taker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-minute-taker/): Takes the minutes after every meeting from the recording and transcript, and sends everyone the same version, updating it when someone points out a correction.
- [Meeting Scheduler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-scheduler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-scheduler/): Books the meeting with a clear purpose and agenda, makes sure it will be recorded and transcribed, and shows up when it starts.
- [Meeting Stand-In](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-stand-in) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-stand-in/): Attends a meeting on your behalf, listens, answers questions within its brief, and hands you a short summary of what happened and what was asked of you.
- [Multi-model Team Room Host](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/multi-model-team-room-host) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/multi-model-team-room-host/): It brings AI models from different vendors into one room as named teammates with their own accounts, makes them take turns so they never talk over each other or set each other off, and adds a new model family by a repeatable sign-up routine.
- [Owner Phone Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant/): It passes the message to the agent team running the work and sends the team's answers back to the phone.
- [Panel Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog/): It watches a panel for spending limits, interrupted turns and idle sessions, pauses instead of forcing a rushed answer, recovers interrupted work in place, and tells you by voice what state the panel is in.
- [Phone-to-Done Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/phone-to-done-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/phone-to-done-assistant/): It passes the instruction to the agent team, turns it into a task for the right teammate, and sends the owner a short note when the task is done.
- [Plan-to-project Packager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/plan-to-project-packager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/plan-to-project-packager/): It takes an agreed decision back to the same panel and turns it into a complete implementation plan, a structured project definition and a briefing deck in web and slide form, checks them for consistency, and leaves the go-ahead to the owner.
- [Project Document Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/project-document-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/project-document-writer/): It writes proper documents inside the conversation, keeps every version, refuses edits based on an out-of-date copy, and finds the files already attached to the conversation.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Social Analytics Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter/): It reports reach and engagement per post and per day, follower growth and tracking-link results across every connected account, X and the Facebook Page.
- [Team AI Colleague](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-ai-colleague) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-ai-colleague/): It is a named AI colleague in the team chat that answers when someone addresses it, in the right thread, already knowing the project's recent history and files.
- [Team Chat Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-chat-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-chat-assistant/): It answers in the same thread using the team's shared memory and documents, and creates or updates documents when asked.
