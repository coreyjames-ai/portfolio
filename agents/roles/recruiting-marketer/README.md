# The Recruiting Marketer

**For a hiring manager with an open role.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/recruiting-marketer/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A job posting becomes a short captioned video that names the role, the team and how to apply. The agent builds the matching cover cards, posts everything to the company's channels and answers the questions candidates leave in the comments. Applications that arrive by email are filed against the role.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Job Ad Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/job-ad-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/job-ad-video-maker/): Turns a job posting into a short captioned vertical video with the role, team and how to apply, and posts it to the company's channels.
- [Carousel and Cover Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer/): Builds carousels, cover cards, hook cards and thumbnails from fixed brand templates, so every slide carries the same type, colours and closing logo.
- [Caption and Format Specialist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist/): Makes every version a channel needs from one master: vertical, square-ish and wide sizes, web and audio formats, captioned and silent copies.
- [Cross-Platform Publisher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher/): It checks a post against each platform's rules, uploads its media, publishes it now or cross-posts it, and withdraws it if needed.
- [Video Mix and Finishing Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor/): Assembles the shots into the cut, adds grain and grade, mixes effects and room tone to a broadcast loudness and checks that no second of the film is silent.
- [Facebook Page Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager/): Uploads photos and videos to the business page, publishes or schedules the post, answers comments and reports how the post did.
- [Paid Social Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/paid-social-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/paid-social-campaign-manager/): It sets up and adjusts paid social campaigns through the ads manager, reads delivery, completion and cost per click, pauses the weaker ad and removes finished campaigns.
- [Candidate Fit Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/candidate-fit-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/candidate-fit-analyst/): Paste in a job description and it tells you, requirement by requirement, whether the candidate's published work covers it, with a link to the page that shows it.

- [Inbound Mail Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/inbound-mail-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/inbound-mail-clerk/): It reads mail sent to the business's addresses, sorts it, answers routine questions, opens a task for anything that needs a person, and notes it on the sender's record.
