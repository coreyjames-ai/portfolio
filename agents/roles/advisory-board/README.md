# The Advisory Board

**For a leadership team facing a hard call.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/advisory-board/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The question goes to a panel of different AI models, each given a role. They argue it in the open, and one of them is assigned to attack whatever the others favour. The panel closes only when every member agrees to the same written text. The agent then files the decision with the reasoning behind it, presents it at the leadership meeting and drafts the announcement.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Speaks to people

- [Board Briefing Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/board-briefing-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/board-briefing-presenter/): It turns an agreed plan into a briefing deck, joins the video meeting as a named digital colleague, presents the deck slide by slide, and drops the plan link into the meeting chat.
- [Decision Announcer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-announcer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-announcer/): It takes a decision the panel agreed, drafts a short public post from the decision document, and puts it into the social posting queue for approval.

### Supervises other agents

- [Feasibility Study Coordinator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/feasibility-study-coordinator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/feasibility-study-coordinator/): It chairs a panel, sends temporary investigators off on side questions, reconciles what they bring back, writes up the questions that need the owner's answer, and drafts a synthesis for the panel to vote on.

### Challenges decisions

- [Virtual Leadership Panel](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/virtual-leadership-panel) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/virtual-leadership-panel/): Gives you a spoken meeting with a facilitator, a technology advisor and a marketing advisor, who take turns, pass questions to each other by name, and respect your interruptions.
- [Decision Panel Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator/): It convenes several AI models as a panel on your question and agrees the goal with you first.
- [Red Team Reviewer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/red-team-reviewer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/red-team-reviewer/): Attacks every major decision and every change of scope from a different model family than the builders, looking for the assumption that breaks, and holds approval until it has answered.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Decision Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-keeper/): Writes down every decision, constraint and open question as it is made, so nobody relitigates last month's call and new people can find the reasoning.

### Audits and guards

- [Panel Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog/): It watches a panel for spending limits, interrupted turns and idle sessions, pauses instead of forcing a rushed answer, recovers interrupted work in place, and tells you by voice what state the panel is in.
