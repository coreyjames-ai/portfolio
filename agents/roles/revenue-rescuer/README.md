# The Revenue Rescuer

**For a finance lead who is tired of losing customers to expired cards.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/revenue-rescuer/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A customer's card fails at 2 a.m. The agent wakes on the failure, reads the customer's history and decides how much the account matters. A small account gets an email and a fresh payment link. A loyal one gets a text, a retention offer and a task on the account owner's list. On Friday the owner gets one number: how much revenue came back.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Payment Recovery Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent/): It sends the buyer a fresh payment link with a short note, watches for the payment, and tells the team whether the sale was recovered.
- [Payment Request Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector/): It sends a client a payment request with a reference and amount, turns on automatic reminders, checks whether it was paid, and withdraws requests that are no longer due.
- [Coupon Drop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager/): It creates a coupon collection for a promotion, loads the codes, sends them in an email or text campaign, and tracks the orders and revenue the coupon brought in.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Subscription Retention Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent/): It moves the customer to the right list, sends a retention or farewell message, and flags valuable cancellations for a person to call.
- [Customer Lifecycle Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent/): It welcomes new buyers, follows their engagement, recovers failed payments, and works to keep subscribers who move to cancel.

### Speaks to people

- [Text Message Dispatcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher/): It sends one-off text messages such as delivery and appointment notices from templates, and reports what was delivered.
- [Sales Follow-up Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant/): It makes sure every conversation has a next step: it creates follow-up tasks with due dates, writes notes on deals, contacts and companies, and keeps the related files.
- [Abandoned Checkout Follow-up](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up/): It finds checkouts that were started but never paid, looks up the customer, and sends one reminder with a fresh payment link.
- [Invoice Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser/): When a payment fails or an invoice goes unpaid it tells the customer with a fresh payment link, reminds them once, and opens a follow-up task for a person.
