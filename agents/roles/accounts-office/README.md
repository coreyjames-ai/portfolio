# The Accounts Office

**For a freelancer, a finance lead or anyone owed money.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/accounts-office/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent sends the payment request, watches for the payment and thanks the customer when it lands. Late invoices get reminders on a schedule. Billing questions are answered from the account record, and the month closes with a revenue report.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Payment Request Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-request-collector/): It sends a client a payment request with a reference and amount, turns on automatic reminders, checks whether it was paid, and withdraws requests that are no longer due.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Subscription Change Handler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler/): When a customer upgrades, downgrades or cancels it updates their record and loyalty status, and for a cancellation sends a retention message and records the reason.
- [Payment Recovery Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-recovery-agent/): It sends the buyer a fresh payment link with a short note, watches for the payment, and tells the team whether the sale was recovered.
- [Payment Setup Specialist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-setup-specialist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-setup-specialist/): It plans how the business will take payment, then creates the products, prices, one-time and subscription payment links and checkout pages with the business's return addresses, and wires the payment notifications.

### Speaks to people

- [Billing Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/billing-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/billing-desk/): It answers customers' billing questions by finding the payment, invoice or subscription, and makes approved changes such as updating a subscription.
- [Invoice Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/invoice-chaser/): When a payment fails or an invoice goes unpaid it tells the customer with a fresh payment link, reminds them once, and opens a follow-up task for a person.
- [Transactional Mail Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/transactional-mail-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/transactional-mail-officer/): It sends receipts, confirmations, password and security notices from approved templates.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Company Records Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/company-records-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/company-records-manager/): It keeps one record per customer company, links the people and deals to it, and imports company lists.
- [Deal File Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk/): Files each document against the right deal, company or contact, finds it on request and removes outdated copies.

### Audits and guards

- [Pricing Change Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog/): It compares the change with the approved offer and alerts the owner when a price or link has drifted.
