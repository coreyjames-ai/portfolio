# The Loyalty Director

**For a retail marketing lead.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/loyalty-director/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent designs the program: the tiers, the rules for earning, the rewards and the limits. Then it runs it, moving members between tiers, answering balance questions and settling redemptions. Members who drift get an offer chosen from what they bought before.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Loyalty Program Architect](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-program-architect) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-program-architect/): It designs and launches the loyalty program: the points currencies and their limits, the tier ladder, the rewards and offers, and publishes the program when it is ready.
- [Points and Redemption Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk/): It credits points for purchases, takes them back on cancellations, and completes reward redemptions so balances are always right.
- [Voucher and Reward Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk/): It issues vouchers to members, redeems or revokes them, checks that a reward is valid, and reports how many codes are left.

### Speaks to people

- [Loyalty Tier Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-tier-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-tier-concierge/): After each order it adds the points, checks whether the customer moved up a tier, and tells them by email or text with what the new tier unlocks.
- [Loyalty Member Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge/): It enrols customers, places them in the right tier, and answers any member's question about their points, tier or history.
- [Win-back Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/win-back-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/win-back-agent/): It finds customers who have gone quiet, gives them a coupon, sends a win-back email or text, and reports who came back and what they spent.
- [SMS Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sms-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sms-campaign-manager/): It creates text campaigns for a list, sends a test to a phone, sends or schedules the campaign, and reports and exports the recipients.
