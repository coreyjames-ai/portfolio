# The Newsletter Editor

**For a team that owes its subscribers something every week.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/newsletter-editor/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent gathers what the company published during the week and lays it out in the newsletter template. It sends a test, then schedules the issue. New subscribers get a confirmation and a welcome. Addresses that bounce or complain come off the list, with the reason recorded.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Email Template Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-template-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-template-designer/): It keeps the library of campaign and transactional templates, previews them with real data, pulls live content from external feeds, and sends test copies before anyone uses a template.
- [Campaign Results Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst/): It reads how each email went, which version of an A/B test won, what each campaign earned in orders, and gives a plain recommendation for the next send.
- [Sending Domain and Sender Steward](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward/): It adds the business's sending domain, gives back the DNS records to publish, checks authentication, and creates and confirms each sender address so mail arrives as the business.

### Speaks to people

- [Newsletter Builder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/newsletter-builder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/newsletter-builder/): It gathers the week's published posts from X, the Facebook Page and other channels, lays them out in the newsletter template, sends a test, and schedules it to the subscriber list.
- [Subscriber Welcome Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscriber-welcome-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscriber-welcome-agent/): It sends the confirmation and welcome messages and adds confirmed contacts to the main audience.
- [Release Notes Mailer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/release-notes-mailer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/release-notes-mailer/): Summarises what changed over a period from the record of completed work and the code it touched, and sends the notes to a subscriber list as an email campaign.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Contact Data Steward](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/contact-data-steward) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/contact-data-steward/): It keeps every contact record complete and current: it creates and updates contacts, adds the fields the business needs, and runs bulk imports and exports when data moves.

### Audits and guards

- [Email List Hygiene Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-list-hygiene-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-list-hygiene-agent/): It removes the address from active lists, records why, and keeps a running view of sender health for the team.
- [Deliverability Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deliverability-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deliverability-keeper/): It watches bounces, spam complaints and blocked addresses, removes bad addresses from lists, clears hard bounces once fixed, and blocks or unblocks whole domains.
