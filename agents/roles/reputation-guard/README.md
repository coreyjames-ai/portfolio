# The Reputation Guard

**For a brand manager or a small business owner.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/reputation-guard/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent hears every mention, tag, comment and inbox message as it arrives. Routine questions get an answer. Abuse is hidden. A complaint with weight behind it goes to the owner's phone with the history attached. If something breaks for customers, the same agent sends the notice by email and text and keeps the public replies consistent with it.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Page Inbox Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector/): It collects new Page comments and messages, and on a slower cycle tagged posts and ratings, so nothing waits for someone to look.
- [Facebook Page Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-page-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-page-analyst/): It reports post and Page performance and how quickly the Page answers people, and it keeps an eye on the Page's usage limits.

### Speaks to people

- [Social Inbox Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-inbox-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-inbox-manager/): It brings comments and mentions from all connected accounts into one inbox and answers each on the platform where it arrived.
- [Facebook Community Responder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-community-responder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-community-responder/): It works the Page's queue of comments, replies, messages, tags and reviews, answers in public or privately, hides spam, and marks each item done.
- [Owner Phone Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant/): It passes the message to the agent team running the work and sends the team's answers back to the phone.
- [Incident Communications Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/incident-communications-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/incident-communications-officer/): During an outage it sends one factual update to affected customers by email and text, posts it on X and the Facebook Page, pins it, messages active website visitors, and sends the all-clear when service returns.
- [X Community Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-community-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-community-manager/): It watches each new post's replies in its first hour, answers questions with facts, likes good replies, quotes worthwhile posts, follows people it talks to, and closes each conversation.
- [Lead Capture Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk/): When someone shows buying interest in a Facebook message, an X direct message, a website chat or an email, it creates the contact and company, opens a deal, sets a follow-up task and answers with a payment link where that fits.

### Audits and guards

- [Reputation Watch Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/reputation-watch-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/reputation-watch-officer/): Several times a day it reads mentions, tags, reviews and live threads about the business, answers what it should, and sends anything urgent to the team's chat.
- [Response Time Guardian](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/response-time-guardian) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/response-time-guardian/): It brings the overdue item to the top, answers it or escalates it to a person, and reports response times across platforms.

## Routes

- [A comment becomes a customer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#a-comment-becomes-a-customer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-comment-becomes-a-customer): The Reputation Guard answers a question under a post. The Lead Catcher opens the deal, the Sales Engineer runs the demo, the Accounts Office collects and the Customer Success Manager sends the welcome.
- [An alert becomes a customer notice](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#an-alert-becomes-a-customer-notice) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#an-alert-becomes-a-customer-notice): The Night Shift contains the fault. The Reputation Guard sends the notice and answers the replies. The Record Keeper records what happened, and the Stand-up Host walks the team through it.
