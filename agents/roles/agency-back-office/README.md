# The Agency Back Office

**For an agency or a company with several brands.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/agency-back-office/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A new client signs. The agent opens their account, sets up the sending domain, connects the social profiles and the payment account, and gives the client's team their access. From then on each brand runs with its own calendar, inboxes and reports, and one person can oversee all of them.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Sending Domain and Sender Steward](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sending-domain-and-sender-steward/): It adds the business's sending domain, gives back the DNS records to publish, checks authentication, and creates and confirms each sender address so mail arrives as the business.
- [Payment Setup Specialist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/payment-setup-specialist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/payment-setup-specialist/): It plans how the business will take payment, then creates the products, prices, one-time and subscription payment links and checkout pages with the business's return addresses, and wires the payment notifications.
- [Social Calendar Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager/): It keeps each brand's posting calendar: brand profiles, which accounts belong to which brand, the weekly posting slots, and every draft and scheduled post, including bulk uploads.
- [Weekly Cross-Channel Brief](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/weekly-cross-channel-brief) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/weekly-cross-channel-brief/): Every week it pulls social, email, sales and payment results into one short brief and sends it to the team's chat, so it reads well on a phone.
- [Import and Export Tracker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/import-and-export-tracker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/import-and-export-tracker/): Follows every background import and export, says when each one finished or failed, and hands back the result file.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Brand Kit Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-kit-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-kit-keeper/): Reads the company website and proposes a brand kit of logo, colours, fonts and social links.

### Runs infrastructure

- [Event Feed Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/event-feed-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/event-feed-manager/): It decides which email, SMS, inbound mail, social and payment events the business wants told about, points them at the right receiver, and checks that they keep arriving.

### Audits and guards

- [Client Account Administrator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/client-account-administrator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/client-account-administrator/): It opens and closes a sub-account for each client or brand, sets each one's plan and features, groups them, and hands the right people access with single sign-on links.
- [Marketing Team Access Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/marketing-team-access-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/marketing-team-access-manager/): It invites colleagues to the email and messaging account, gives each the permissions their job needs, and removes access when someone leaves.
- [Social Account Health Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer/): It checks every connected account's sign-in, permissions and usage limits and warns before a credential or allowance runs out.
