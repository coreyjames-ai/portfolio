# The Message Centre

**For a customer team whose customers live on their phones.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/message-centre/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Receipts, confirmations and security notices go out from approved templates. Text and chat-app campaigns are written, tested on a real phone and sent to a list with consent on file. When a customer says a message never arrived, the agent looks up that one message and its delivery history. What customers do is recorded against their records, so the next message fits.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Speaks to people

- [Transactional Mail Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/transactional-mail-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/transactional-mail-officer/): It sends receipts, confirmations, password and security notices from approved templates.
- [SMS Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sms-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sms-campaign-manager/): It creates text campaigns for a list, sends a test to a phone, sends or schedules the campaign, and reports and exports the recipients.
- [WhatsApp Business Messenger](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/whatsapp-business-messenger) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/whatsapp-business-messenger/): It writes message templates, submits them for approval, runs WhatsApp campaigns and sends individual messages, then reads the delivery report.
- [Text Message Dispatcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher/): It sends one-off text messages such as delivery and appointment notices from templates, and reports what was delivered.
- [Launch Campaign Runner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner/): From one brief it sends the launch email, the launch text message and WhatsApp template, posts on X and the Facebook Page, cross-posts to other channels, pins the announcement and opens a payment link.
- [Back-in-Stock Notifier](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/back-in-stock-notifier) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/back-in-stock-notifier/): It signs customers up for an alert on a product they want, and tells them when it returns.
- [Order Status Notifier](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/order-status-notifier) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/order-status-notifier/): It sends order updates to customers in the website chat, by text or by email, so fewer customers write in to ask.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Contact Activity Recorder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/contact-activity-recorder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/contact-activity-recorder/): It records what customers do (sign-ups, purchases, feature use) against their records so segments and automated journeys can react to it.
- [List and Segment Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/list-and-segment-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/list-and-segment-keeper/): It keeps mailing lists and segments tidy, adds and removes people, organises lists into folders, and reports who is on each list.

### Audits and guards

- [Consent and Opt-in Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer/): It signs new subscribers up through a confirm-by-email step, records where consent came from, honours every unsubscribe and block, and keeps an export for anyone who asks what is held.
