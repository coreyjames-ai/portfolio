# The Support Desk

**For a support lead, or a founder who answers customers alone.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/support-desk/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A request arrives by email, chat or a social inbox. The agent opens a case and looks for the answer in the help content, past cases and the product's own code. It replies in the channel the customer used. If the problem is a defect, it files the issue for engineering with the evidence attached and tells the customer when the fix ships. Response times are tracked against the target.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Issue Triage Engineer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/issue-triage-engineer/): Reads each new issue, finds the code most likely at fault and comments the suspect file and line with a triage label so a developer starts in the right place.
- [Help Article Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker/): Turns a help article into a short captioned how-to video so customers can watch the fix instead of reading it.

### Speaks to people

- [Customer Support Desk Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent/): It opens a case, hands it to the right specialist, writes back to the customer by email, and closes the case with a note of what was done.
- [Support Inbox Triage Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent/): It reads new support emails, chat messages and social messages, finds the answer in the business's knowledge base, drafts the reply for a person to approve, sends it and notes the case on the customer's record.
- [Phone and Call Receptionist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist/): Answers direct calls to the AI teammate, listens, replies in a natural voice, and stops talking the moment the caller interrupts.
- [Facebook Community Responder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-community-responder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-community-responder/): It works the Page's queue of comments, replies, messages, tags and reviews, answers in public or privately, hides spam, and marks each item done.
- [Billing Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/billing-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/billing-desk/): It answers customers' billing questions by finding the payment, invoice or subscription, and makes approved changes such as updating a subscription.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Team Knowledge Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk/): Answers questions in team chat from everything that team is allowed to see: the channel's own conversation, files shared there, the codebase, reference documents, libraries, past engineering notes and agent activity.
- [Incident Code Locator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/incident-code-locator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/incident-code-locator/): Finds the code that produced the error, traces what depends on it, explains the failure path, and leaves the finding in the team lead's inbox.

### Audits and guards

- [Response Time Guardian](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/response-time-guardian) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/response-time-guardian/): It brings the overdue item to the top, answers it or escalates it to a person, and reports response times across platforms.

## Routes

- [A complaint becomes a release](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#a-complaint-becomes-a-release) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-complaint-becomes-a-release): The Support Desk finds a defect and files it.
