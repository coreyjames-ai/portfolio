# The Launch Manager

**For a founder with a product shipping on Friday.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/launch-manager/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent researches what buyers are saying, writes the announcement for every channel and produces the launch film with narration and cutdowns. It emails the customer list, texts the customers who asked to be first and presents the deck at the team meeting. On the day it answers comments and mentions, issues the launch coupon and watches the payments come in. On Monday it reports the results.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Short Film Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/short-film-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/short-film-producer/): Takes an approved story and returns a finished short film with sound, plus a version sized for phones.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.
- [Social Video Cutdown Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-video-cutdown-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-video-cutdown-editor/): Turns one finished video into feed versions: a cold open, a size-capped upload, a looping GIF and a carousel built from its best frames.
- [Cross-Platform Publisher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher/): It checks a post against each platform's rules, uploads its media, publishes it now or cross-posts it, and withdraws it if needed.
- [Coupon Drop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager/): It creates a coupon collection for a promotion, loads the codes, sends them in an email or text campaign, and tracks the orders and revenue the coupon brought in.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.

### Speaks to people

- [Launch Campaign Runner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/launch-campaign-runner/): From one brief it sends the launch email, the launch text message and WhatsApp template, posts on X and the Facebook Page, cross-posts to other channels, pins the announcement and opens a payment link.
- [Email Campaign Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/email-campaign-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/email-campaign-manager/): It builds each campaign from a template for a chosen list or segment, sends a test, schedules or sends it, and files it in the right folder.
- [Text Message Dispatcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/text-message-dispatcher/): It sends one-off text messages such as delivery and appointment notices from templates, and reports what was delivered.
- [Slide Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/slide-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/slide-presenter/): Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.
- [Social Community Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-community-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-community-manager/): It reads the thread, replies in the brand voice, and records each reply with the post it belongs to.

### Audits and guards

- [Post Launch Monitor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor/): It confirms where the post went live, re-posts to any platform that failed, and gives the owner the live links.
