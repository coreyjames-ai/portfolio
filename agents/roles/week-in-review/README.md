# The Week in Review

**For a founder who wants Friday's numbers without building the report.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/week-in-review/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Each week the agent pulls the results from every social channel, the email campaigns, the sales pipeline and the payment account. It writes a short brief that reads well on a phone and posts it in the team chat. It also turns the numbers into a designed reel over the week's most-watched clips and schedules it.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Weekly Cross-Channel Brief](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/weekly-cross-channel-brief) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/weekly-cross-channel-brief/): Every week it pulls social, email, sales and payment results into one short brief and sends it to the team's chat, so it reads well on a phone.
- [Weekly Results Reel Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/weekly-results-reel-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/weekly-results-reel-maker/): Pulls the week's post results from each network, turns the numbers into designed cards over the week's best clips and schedules the reel.
- [Social Analytics Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter/): It reports reach and engagement per post and per day, follower growth and tracking-link results across every connected account, X and the Facebook Page.
- [X Performance Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-performance-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-performance-analyst/): It reports what was posted and how each post did over time, and how much of the monthly read allowance is left.
- [Facebook Page Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-page-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-page-analyst/): It reports post and Page performance and how quickly the Page answers people, and it keeps an eye on the Page's usage limits.
- [Campaign Results Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst/): It reads how each email went, which version of an A/B test won, what each campaign earned in orders, and gives a plain recommendation for the next send.
- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Motion Graphics Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/motion-graphics-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/motion-graphics-artist/): Turns a still into a short moving background and sets kinetic type over it, so the post moves from the first frame.
- [Revenue Attribution Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-attribution-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-attribution-analyst/): It shows which email campaigns, text campaigns and automated journeys brought in orders and revenue, and which products sold through them.
