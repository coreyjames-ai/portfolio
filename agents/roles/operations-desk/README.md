# The Operations Desk

**For a founder who needs a function staffed around the clock, such as support.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/operations-desk/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The founder describes the function once. The agent sets up a standing desk with a lead and a roster of specialists who start only when there is work. An event arrives from the business's own software, such as a failed import or a cancelled booking. The lead opens a task, brings the right specialist online, checks the result and stands the specialist down again.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Long-Running Operations Planner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/long-running-operations-planner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/long-running-operations-planner/): Sets up a standing operation with open-ended responsibilities, a roster of specialists who start only when needed, and a lead who starts first.

### Speaks to people

- [Platform Help Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/platform-help-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/platform-help-desk/): Registers itself before any restart, watches progress every fifteen minutes, answers the team lead's questions on a private support channel, logs real defects and ships the smallest fix when something blocks the team.

### Supervises other agents

- [Operations Desk Lead](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/operations-desk-lead) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/operations-desk-lead/): Watches for incoming events, opens a finite task for each one, brings the right specialist online, closes the task and stands the specialist down again.
- [Agent Workforce Provisioner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/agent-workforce-provisioner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/agent-workforce-provisioner/): Starts agents from role definitions in locked-down containers, pauses, resumes or stops them, and reloads the role and tool catalogs when they change.
- [Agent Coordination Dispatcher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/agent-coordination-dispatcher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/agent-coordination-dispatcher/): It acts on the request, starts a child job when the work needs another specialist, and replies to the sender.
- [Agent Team Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/agent-team-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/agent-team-designer/): Takes a project's goal and its source material, explores that material through the search tools, proposes a roster of specialist agents, and writes their skill guides and agent definitions.

### Runs infrastructure

- [Application Event Responder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/application-event-responder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/application-event-responder/): It decides what the event needs, starts that work with the right team member, and checks the result when it comes back.
- [Background Job Orchestrator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/background-job-orchestrator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/background-job-orchestrator/): Splits a job into child jobs, hands them to short-lived workers, tracks each one and collects the results.
- [Business Event Switchboard](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/business-event-switchboard) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/business-event-switchboard/): It connects payments, email, social activity, inbound mail and the website so every customer signal lands in one team inbox, where it can become a task.
