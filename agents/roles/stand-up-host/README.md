# The Stand-up Host

**For an engineering or operations team.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/stand-up-host/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Each morning the agent gathers what every person and every AI worker finished, what is blocked and what changed overnight. It runs the stand-up, writes the notes and sends the project status to the people who asked to be kept informed.

## Function

[Meetings and teamwork](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/meetings-and-teamwork/): Agents that sit in the meeting, speak, present and follow through.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Speaks to people

- [Daily Standup Host](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/daily-standup-host) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/daily-standup-host/): Runs the morning standup: it calls on each person, notes blockers against the right project, and posts the summary when the meeting ends.
- [Project Status Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/project-status-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/project-status-reporter/): Reads where each project stands and gives a short spoken update in the meeting, with a written version in the meeting chat.
- [Action-Item Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser/): Pulls every promise made in a meeting into a list with owners and due dates, then nudges each owner by chat until the item is done.
- [Meeting Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator/): Keeps a large call orderly: it notices raised hands, calls on people by name in the order they asked, and keeps track of who is speaking and who has dropped off.

### Supervises other agents

- [Close-out and Pause Coordinator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/close-out-and-pause-coordinator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/close-out-and-pause-coordinator/): It briefs you on where work stands after a break, checks the final evidence before closing a task, phase or project, pauses a team cleanly without losing work, and restarts a stuck worker.

### Challenges decisions

- [Delivery Review Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/delivery-review-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/delivery-review-presenter/): Joins the review meeting, shares and walks through the project's closing summary, raises a hand for questions and posts the summary in the chat.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Stand-up Note Taker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/stand-up-note-taker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/stand-up-note-taker/): Sits in the stand-up, captures the transcript and recording, lines up what was said with each project's status digest and sends the action items to the team lead.
