# The Social Desk

**For a social media manager with a full calendar and no spare hands.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/social-desk/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent works from the posting calendar. A slot with no approved image or video starts production, and carousels and covers come from the brand templates. Posts go out at the hours the audience is active. When a post fails on one network, the agent reads why, retries it and hands back the live links. Comments and messages are collected every couple of minutes so none sit unseen.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Content Calendar Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer/): Works from the posting calendar so every upcoming slot has an approved, checked image or video, starts production when one is missing and schedules the post on each network.
- [Carousel and Cover Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/carousel-and-cover-designer/): Builds carousels, cover cards, hook cards and thumbnails from fixed brand templates, so every slide carries the same type, colours and closing logo.
- [Best-Time Scheduler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/best-time-scheduler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/best-time-scheduler/): It reads when the audience is most active and moves the posting slots to match.
- [Scheduled Post Publisher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/scheduled-post-publisher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/scheduled-post-publisher/): It publishes posts that have come due, cancels withdrawn ones, and sends any waiting social notifications.
- [X Publishing Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-publishing-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-publishing-desk/): It posts the day's pieces with their images or video at their times, keeps the queue, and removes and redoes its own mistakes.
- [Facebook Page Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/facebook-page-manager/): Uploads photos and videos to the business page, publishes or schedules the post, answers comments and reports how the post did.
- [Failed Post Retry Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/failed-post-retry-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/failed-post-retry-officer/): It finds posts that failed to go out, reads why, retries one or all, checks the account's health and tells the team about anything it cannot fix.
- [Page Inbox Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector/): It collects new Page comments and messages, and on a slower cycle tagged posts and ratings, so nothing waits for someone to look.
- [Media Drop-off Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/media-drop-off-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/media-drop-off-desk/): Sends the owner a private upload page, waits for the photos or video to arrive, checks them against each platform's limits and attaches them to the waiting post.

### Audits and guards

- [Post Launch Monitor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/post-launch-monitor/): It confirms where the post went live, re-posts to any platform that failed, and gives the owner the live links.
- [Social Account Health Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-account-health-officer/): It checks every connected account's sign-in, permissions and usage limits and warns before a credential or allowance runs out.
