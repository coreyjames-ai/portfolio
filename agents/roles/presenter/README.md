# The Presenter

**For a founder with a recurring update to give.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/presenter/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent builds the deck from the documents the team already trusts. It joins the meeting, raises its hand, presents slide by slide and takes questions by voice. Afterwards it cuts the recording into a narrated highlight video for the people who missed it.

## Function

[Meetings and teamwork](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/meetings-and-teamwork/): Agents that sit in the meeting, speak, present and follow through.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Document Writer and Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor/): Drafts a plan, checklist or report in the team chat, revises it when asked, keeps every earlier version, and retires documents that are out of date.
- [Meeting Highlight Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-highlight-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-highlight-editor/): Turns a meeting recording into a short highlight clip with a narrated summary and posts it for people who missed the call.
- [Weekly Results Reel Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/weekly-results-reel-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/weekly-results-reel-maker/): Pulls the week's post results from each network, turns the numbers into designed cards over the week's best clips and schedules the reel.

### Speaks to people

- [Slide Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/slide-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/slide-presenter/): Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.
- [Meeting Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-moderator/): Keeps a large call orderly: it notices raised hands, calls on people by name in the order they asked, and keeps track of who is speaking and who has dropped off.
- [Board Briefing Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/board-briefing-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/board-briefing-presenter/): It turns an agreed plan into a briefing deck, joins the video meeting as a named digital colleague, presents the deck slide by slide, and drops the plan link into the meeting chat.
- [Meeting Recap Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-recap-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-recap-producer/): Joins a meeting as its own participant, keeps the transcript, and after the call returns a short narrated recap video with captions, posting the link in the meeting chat.
- [Live Demo Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/live-demo-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/live-demo-presenter/): Runs a live walkthrough in a video meeting, sharing a browser and a terminal, typing as people watch, and explaining each step aloud.
