# The Lead Catcher

**For a sales team that loses inquiries.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/lead-catcher/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

An inquiry arrives through the website, an email, a chat or a direct message. The agent creates the contact, the company and the deal, reads what it can about the company and sends a reply that answers the question asked. It sets the follow-up task, sends the reminder when the day comes and moves the deal through the pipeline. On Monday the sales lead gets the pipeline review.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Sales Pipeline Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager/): It keeps every deal in the right stage of the right pipeline, links deals to the people and companies involved, imports deals from other tools, and reports the pipeline.
- [Page Inbox Collector](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/page-inbox-collector/): It collects new Page comments and messages, and on a slower cycle tagged posts and ratings, so nothing waits for someone to look.

### Speaks to people

- [Lead Capture Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/lead-capture-desk/): When someone shows buying interest in a Facebook message, an X direct message, a website chat or an email, it creates the contact and company, opens a deal, sets a follow-up task and answers with a payment link where that fits.
- [Inbound Mail Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/inbound-mail-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/inbound-mail-clerk/): It reads mail sent to the business's addresses, sorts it, answers routine questions, opens a task for anything that needs a person, and notes it on the sender's record.
- [Website Chat Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent/): It answers visitors in the website chat as a named agent, corrects or removes its own messages, and sends proactive messages such as order updates or announcements to one visitor or a batch.
- [Sales Follow-up Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-follow-up-assistant/): It makes sure every conversation has a next step: it creates follow-up tasks with due dates, writes notes on deals, contacts and companies, and keeps the related files.
- [X Direct Message Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/x-direct-message-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/x-direct-message-desk/): It reads direct messages and, for approved outreach, sends messages only from a checked preview of recipients and text.
- [Campaign Follow-up Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/campaign-follow-up-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/campaign-follow-up-agent/): It moves engaged readers into a follow-up sequence and hands the most engaged ones to sales.
- [Website Visitor Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/website-visitor-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/website-visitor-assistant/): On any page of the site it gives the visitor's assistant ready-made actions for a fit report, a search, a page summary, an introduction and a message to the owner.

### Challenges decisions

- [Pipeline Weekly Reviewer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/pipeline-weekly-reviewer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/pipeline-weekly-reviewer/): Each week it lists deals that have stalled and tasks that are overdue, writes a note on each, and sends the summary to the team's chat.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Company Records Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/company-records-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/company-records-manager/): It keeps one record per customer company, links the people and deals to it, and imports company lists.
