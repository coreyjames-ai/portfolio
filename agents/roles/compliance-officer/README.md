# The Compliance Officer

**For whoever has to answer for what was done.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/compliance-officer/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent can show what any AI worker did, in order, and who approved it. It keeps consent records for every contact, checks each piece of content against the brand rules before it goes out and records where every image and clip came from.

## Function

[Compliance and trust](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/compliance-and-trust/): A record of who did what, checked against the rules.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Speaks to people

- [Bounce and Unsubscribe Responder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/bounce-and-unsubscribe-responder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/bounce-and-unsubscribe-responder/): When an email bounces, is marked as spam or gets an unsubscribe, it takes that person off the marketing lists at once and notes why on their record.

### Audits and guards

- [Agent Activity Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/agent-activity-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/agent-activity-auditor/): Reconstructs what any agent did in a time window: its raw actions, the conversations it took part in and the money it spent, in one audit report.
- [Record-Keeping Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/record-keeping-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/record-keeping-auditor/): Checks that meetings were set to record before they began, that recordings and transcripts exist, and that key documents have a complete version history, then writes up any gaps.
- [Consent and Opt-in Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/consent-and-opt-in-officer/): It signs new subscribers up through a confirm-by-email step, records where consent came from, honours every unsubscribe and block, and keeps an export for anyone who asks what is held.
- [Brand Compliance Checker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-compliance-checker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-compliance-checker/): Checks every asset before it goes out: only approved words on screen, no web addresses or slide numbers, readable contrast, and the right size and format for the channel.
- [Asset Origin Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/asset-origin-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/asset-origin-auditor/): Answers "where did this image come from": which source files, which AI models and which settings produced each published asset, and whether the file is unchanged.
- [Team Handbook Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-handbook-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-handbook-auditor/): This agent reads the whole team handbook and every automated routine, finds rules that nobody actually enforces, and drafts the changes for the next revision.
- [Data Lineage Investigator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/data-lineage-investigator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/data-lineage-investigator/): This agent traces a record back through every job and change that touched it and writes up the answer.
- [Operations Audit Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/operations-audit-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/operations-audit-clerk/): Keeps the trail of every pause, resume, scale and stop, and answers who did what to which job and when.
- [Secret Access Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/secret-access-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/secret-access-auditor/): Lists every stored credential with the roles and machines allowed to use it.
