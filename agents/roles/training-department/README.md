# The Training Department

**For a support or training lead.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/training-department/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Help articles and product pages become short narrated videos. The agent cuts demo clips from a live screen and records audio briefings for staff who are on the road. When a page changes, its video is remade. The same script can be voiced in other languages.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Help Article Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/help-article-video-maker/): Turns a help article into a short captioned how-to video so customers can watch the fix instead of reading it.
- [Explainer Video Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/explainer-video-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/explainer-video-maker/): Turns a written page or article into a short narrated explainer with designed cards, captions and moving backgrounds, ready to post with the link in the text.
- [Product Demo Clip Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/product-demo-clip-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/product-demo-clip-maker/): Records a terminal or product session, masks addresses and private details, paces it and returns a captioned demo clip ready to post.
- [Dubbing and Translation Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/dubbing-and-translation-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/dubbing-and-translation-producer/): Turns one narrated video into versions in other languages, each with its own narration, captions and word-for-word check in that language.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.
- [Meeting Highlight Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-highlight-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-highlight-editor/): Turns a meeting recording into a short highlight clip with a narrated summary and posts it for people who missed the call.
- [Storyboard Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist/): Turns a story into a shot-by-shot plan with timing, camera moves, the few lines of on-screen text and how each shot will be made.
- [On-Screen Content Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/on-screen-content-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/on-screen-content-designer/): Builds realistic but invented screens, terminals and dashboards for videos so no real log, customer name or address ever appears on camera.

### Speaks to people

- [Audio Briefing Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer/): Turns the week's project activity into a short narrated audio briefing that people can listen to on the move.
