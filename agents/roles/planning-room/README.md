# The Planning Room

**For a founder or manager deciding what to build next.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/planning-room/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Someone asks in team chat whether an idea is worth building. The agent brings several AI models into the room to debate it and puts the open questions to the owner while everyone is still there. Once the owner approves, the same panel turns the decision into a full plan, a project definition and a briefing deck. If the discussion runs long, a watchdog pauses it at the spending limit and says where things stand.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Idea-to-Approved-Plan Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/idea-to-approved-plan-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/idea-to-approved-plan-agent/): It runs a planning debate across several AI models, asks the owner to settle the open questions, and after the owner approves, starts a team on the plan.
- [Plan-to-project Packager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/plan-to-project-packager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/plan-to-project-packager/): It takes an agreed decision back to the same panel and turns it into a complete implementation plan, a structured project definition and a briefing deck in web and slide form, checks them for consistency, and leaves the go-ahead to the owner.
- [Project Planning Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/project-planning-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/project-planning-clerk/): It turns what you want done into a fully specified plan grounded in the real code and project, checks it before anyone begins, records the facts it relied on with two independent checks, and handles approved changes to a plan already under way.
- [Project Document Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/project-document-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/project-document-writer/): It writes proper documents inside the conversation, keeps every version, refuses edits based on an out-of-date copy, and finds the files already attached to the conversation.
- [Research Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/research-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/research-assistant/): Finds and reads the relevant files and web pages, searches them, recalls related past work and returns a structured summary.

### Challenges decisions

- [Planning Panel Facilitator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/planning-panel-facilitator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/planning-panel-facilitator/): It brings several AI models together to debate the plan, asks the owner to decide the open questions in the room, and hands back a plan ready for approval.
- [Expert Review Panel](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/expert-review-panel) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/expert-review-panel/): Puts a question to several AI models at once, has them propose, challenge and revise in turns, and returns a written record of where they agree, where they differ, and what needs your decision.
- [Decision Panel Moderator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-panel-moderator/): It convenes several AI models as a panel on your question and agrees the goal with you first.

### Audits and guards

- [Panel Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/panel-watchdog/): It watches a panel for spending limits, interrupted turns and idle sessions, pauses instead of forcing a rushed answer, recovers interrupted work in place, and tells you by voice what state the panel is in.
