# The Front Desk

**For a small business with a phone that rings.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/front-desk/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent answers calls and chats in a natural voice, at the pace of a conversation. It answers from what the company knows, takes a message when it cannot and sends anything urgent to the owner's phone.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Speaks to people

- [Phone and Call Receptionist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/phone-and-call-receptionist/): Answers direct calls to the AI teammate, listens, replies in a natural voice, and stops talking the moment the caller interrupts.
- [Chat Front Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/chat-front-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/chat-front-desk/): Answers questions people send it in chat, one to one or during a meeting, and replies in the same conversation.
- [Spoken Team Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/spoken-team-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/spoken-team-assistant/): It answers a spoken question with a short reply out loud and posts the full written answer in the right chat room.
- [Owner Phone Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/owner-phone-assistant/): It passes the message to the agent team running the work and sends the team's answers back to the phone.
- [Hands-Free Office Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/hands-free-office-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/hands-free-office-assistant/): Takes spoken requests at the desk speakerphone, answers quick questions aloud, and hands longer jobs, such as building a presentation, to a background worker while showing progress in the team chat.
- [Customer Support Desk Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-support-desk-agent/): It opens a case, hands it to the right specialist, writes back to the customer by email, and closes the case with a note of what was done.
- [Support Inbox Triage Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/support-inbox-triage-agent/): It reads new support emails, chat messages and social messages, finds the answer in the business's knowledge base, drafts the reply for a person to approve, sends it and notes the case on the customer's record.
- [Fast Data Lookup Assistant](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/fast-data-lookup-assistant) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/fast-data-lookup-assistant/): This agent answers questions about another app's records from a kept-in-sync copy, so the team gets fast answers and the other app is not overloaded.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Team Knowledge Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-knowledge-desk/): Answers questions in team chat from everything that team is allowed to see: the channel's own conversation, files shared there, the codebase, reference documents, libraries, past engineering notes and agent activity.
