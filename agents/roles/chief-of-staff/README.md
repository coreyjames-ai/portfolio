# The Chief of Staff

**For a founder with more meetings than hours.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/chief-of-staff/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent attends the meetings the founder cannot, speaks when it is asked a question and presents the slides when its turn comes. Afterwards it writes the minutes, sends each attendee their action items and chases the ones that slip. It updates the deal records with what the customer said and redrafts the proposal to match. The next morning it reads the founder a five-minute spoken briefing.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Sales Pipeline Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sales-pipeline-manager/): It keeps every deal in the right stage of the right pipeline, links deals to the people and companies involved, imports deals from other tools, and reports the pipeline.
- [Document Writer and Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/document-writer-and-editor/): Drafts a plan, checklist or report in the team chat, revises it when asked, keeps every earlier version, and retires documents that are out of date.

### Speaks to people

- [Meeting Stand-In](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-stand-in) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-stand-in/): Attends a meeting on your behalf, listens, answers questions within its brief, and hands you a short summary of what happened and what was asked of you.
- [Action-Item Chaser](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/action-item-chaser/): Pulls every promise made in a meeting into a list with owners and due dates, then nudges each owner by chat until the item is done.
- [Slide Presenter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/slide-presenter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/slide-presenter/): Turns a written project document into a slide deck, presents it in the meeting, moves through the slides as it talks, and shares the deck once the meeting is over.
- [Meeting Recap Sender](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-recap-sender) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-recap-sender/): Sends each attendee a personal recap after the meeting, covering what was decided, what they own, and where to find the recording.
- [Audio Briefing Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer/): Turns the week's project activity into a short narrated audio briefing that people can listen to on the move.
- [Meeting Scheduler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-scheduler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-scheduler/): Books the meeting with a clear purpose and agenda, makes sure it will be recorded and transcribed, and shows up when it starts.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Meeting Minute-Taker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-minute-taker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-minute-taker/): Takes the minutes after every meeting from the recording and transcript, and sends everyone the same version, updating it when someone points out a correction.
- [Decision Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-keeper/): Writes down every decision, constraint and open question as it is made, so nobody relitigates last month's call and new people can find the reasoning.

### Audits and guards

- [Record-Keeping Auditor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/record-keeping-auditor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/record-keeping-auditor/): Checks that meetings were set to record before they began, that recordings and transcripts exist, and that key documents have a complete version history, then writes up any gaps.
