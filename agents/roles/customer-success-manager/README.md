# The Customer Success Manager

**For a subscription business.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/customer-success-manager/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A first payment arrives and the agent sends the welcome, enrols the customer in the loyalty program and schedules the check-ins. It notices when a customer goes quiet and reaches out before the renewal. Upgrades, downgrades and cancellations each get their own message and the matching change to the account.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Subscription Change Handler](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-change-handler/): When a customer upgrades, downgrades or cancels it updates their record and loyalty status, and for a cancellation sends a retention message and records the reason.
- [Subscription Retention Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/subscription-retention-agent/): It moves the customer to the right list, sends a retention or farewell message, and flags valuable cancellations for a person to call.
- [Customer Lifecycle Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/customer-lifecycle-agent/): It welcomes new buyers, follows their engagement, recovers failed payments, and works to keep subscribers who move to cancel.

### Speaks to people

- [New Customer Welcome Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/new-customer-welcome-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/new-customer-welcome-agent/): After a first purchase it adds the customer to the welcome list, sends the welcome email, enrols them in the loyalty program, marks the deal won and books a check-in.
- [Loyalty Member Concierge](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/loyalty-member-concierge/): It enrols customers, places them in the right tier, and answers any member's question about their points, tier or history.
- [Win-back Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/win-back-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/win-back-agent/): It finds customers who have gone quiet, gives them a coupon, sends a win-back email or text, and reports who came back and what they spent.
- [WhatsApp Business Messenger](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/whatsapp-business-messenger) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/whatsapp-business-messenger/): It writes message templates, submits them for approval, runs WhatsApp campaigns and sends individual messages, then reads the delivery report.

### Keeps knowledge

- [Custom Records Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/custom-records-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/custom-records-keeper/): Keeps the records the business defines for itself, such as vehicles, policies or properties, links each to its customer, and updates or removes them in bulk.
- [Deal File Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/deal-file-clerk/): Files each document against the right deal, company or contact, finds it on request and removes outdated copies.
- [Contact Activity Recorder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/contact-activity-recorder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/contact-activity-recorder/): It records what customers do (sign-ups, purchases, feature use) against their records so segments and automated journeys can react to it.

## Routes

- [A comment becomes a customer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#a-comment-becomes-a-customer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-comment-becomes-a-customer): The Reputation Guard answers a question under a post. The Lead Catcher opens the deal, the Sales Engineer runs the demo, the Accounts Office collects and the Customer Success Manager sends the welcome.
- [A failed payment becomes a kept account](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#a-failed-payment-becomes-a-kept-account) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#a-failed-payment-becomes-a-kept-account): The Revenue Rescuer recovers the payment. The Customer Success Manager checks in, and the Loyalty Director moves the customer up a tier.
