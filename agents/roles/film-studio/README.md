# The Film Studio

**For a marketing lead with a story and no crew.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/film-studio/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

A one-paragraph brief becomes a storyboard. The agent generates each shot, turns product photos into motion and builds the on-screen graphics. It records the narration, lays in sound, cuts the film and exports a version for every channel with captions. A reviewer sees each cut before it goes out.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Production Planner](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/production-planner) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/production-planner/): Turns an approved storyboard into a signed-off production plan with the GPU budget it needs and a brief for each specialist, so production starts without back-and-forth.
- [Storyboard Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/storyboard-artist/): Turns a story into a shot-by-shot plan with timing, camera moves, the few lines of on-screen text and how each shot will be made.
- [Photo-to-Motion Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/photo-to-motion-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/photo-to-motion-artist/): Turns a real photograph into a moving camera shot without repainting it, so the real room and hardware stay exactly as they are.
- [3D Scene Builder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/3d-scene-builder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/3d-scene-builder/): Builds the world of a film as a scripted 3D scene with exact moving parts and camera paths, and renders the picture layers the finishing team needs.
- [Motion Graphics Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/motion-graphics-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/motion-graphics-artist/): Turns a still into a short moving background and sets kinetic type over it, so the post moves from the first frame.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.
- [Sound Designer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/sound-designer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/sound-designer/): Gives every shot its own sound effects generated from the picture and a short sound brief, cut to the exact shot length.
- [Video Mix and Finishing Editor](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/video-mix-and-finishing-editor/): Assembles the shots into the cut, adds grain and grade, mixes effects and room tone to a broadcast loudness and checks that no second of the film is silent.
- [Caption and Format Specialist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/caption-and-format-specialist/): Makes every version a channel needs from one master: vertical, square-ish and wide sizes, web and audio formats, captioned and silent copies.

### Challenges decisions

- [Creative Review Coordinator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/creative-review-coordinator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/creative-review-coordinator/): Collects every candidate image and video with what it is meant to do, removes the ones turned down, records which one was picked for each post and hands the picked file to publishing.

### Runs infrastructure

- [Render Farm Operator](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/render-farm-operator) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/render-farm-operator/): Keeps the GPU image and video service running for the whole team: starts it only within the spend limit, watches health and logs, frees GPU memory between jobs and shuts it down when work is done.
- [Render Job Troubleshooter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/render-job-troubleshooter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/render-job-troubleshooter/): Steps in when an image or video job fails: reads the error and the service log, re-sends the finished file if only the hand-off failed, otherwise fixes the cause and runs the job again.
