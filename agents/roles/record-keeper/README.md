# The Record Keeper

**For whoever owns a system now.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/record-keeper/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

"Why is it built this way?" has an answer. The agent keeps a searchable record of what each working session did, what was decided and why. When something that used to work stops, it finds the change that broke it.

## Function

[Engineering](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/engineering/): Planning, building, reviewing and releasing software, with the reasoning kept.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Keeps knowledge

- [System Historian](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/system-historian) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/system-historian/): Reconstructs a system's story, covering the decisions that shaped it, the changes made, the problems hit and how each was fixed, and the trade-offs accepted, in time order.
- [Decision History Keeper](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-history-keeper) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-history-keeper/): It turns notable actions into entries in the searchable decision history.
- [Session Recorder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/session-recorder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/session-recorder/): Opens a record when a session starts, captures the questions asked and the answers given, tracks which model was used, and writes a summary when the session ends.
- [Engineering Notetaker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/engineering-notetaker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/engineering-notetaker/): Turns the team's working sessions into typed notes.
- [Decision Recorder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/decision-recorder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/decision-recorder/): Watches the team's decisions log and working notes folder.
- [Regression Historian](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/regression-historian) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/regression-historian/): Given a broken function or an error, works out which pieces of work touched that area and in what order, who calls it now, and how the failure travels through the code.
- [Team Memory Search Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/team-memory-search-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/team-memory-search-desk/): Searches past notes by keyword, by meaning or by both, filtered by project and date.
- [Meeting Decision Recorder](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/meeting-decision-recorder) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/meeting-decision-recorder/): After the meeting it takes the transcript, has a panel reconcile what was decided and what is still open, records each decision in the project's memory, and posts the minutes as a document.

## Routes

- [An alert becomes a customer notice](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#an-alert-becomes-a-customer-notice) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#an-alert-becomes-a-customer-notice): The Night Shift contains the fault. The Reputation Guard sends the notice and answers the replies. The Record Keeper records what happened, and the Stand-up Host walks the team through it.
- [An outage becomes a lesson](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/routes#an-outage-becomes-a-lesson) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/routes/#an-outage-becomes-a-lesson): The Night Shift contains it and the Incident Commander plans the fix. The Record Keeper files what happened and the Handbook Keeper updates the playbook.
