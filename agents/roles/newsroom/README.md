# The Newsroom

**For a brand with something to say every day and nobody to say it.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/newsroom/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent reads what the market is arguing about, picks the angle and writes it for each channel. It produces the images, the short film and the voice-over, schedules the posts and answers the comments as they arrive. The week's numbers choose next week's topics.

## Function

[Marketing and audience](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/marketing-and-audience/): Films, posts, campaigns and replies, from a brief to a published result.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Trend Response Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/trend-response-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/trend-response-writer/): When a conversation takes off it writes a short post or reply that adds a fact from the business's own record, has a person approve it and publishes it.
- [Weekly Content Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/weekly-content-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/weekly-content-producer/): It plans and produces a week of LinkedIn and X posts: it researches what is live, checks the facts, picks a theme with the owner, writes per platform, edits to the house voice, renders images or short video, and puts everything in front of the owner to approve.
- [Brand Image Maker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-image-maker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-image-maker/): Turns an image brief into finished images in the brand's look, with the words set by a designer template instead of drawn by the image model.
- [Short Film Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/short-film-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/short-film-producer/): Takes an approved story and returns a finished short film with sound, plus a version sized for phones.
- [Voice-Over Artist](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voice-over-artist/): Reads a script in a chosen voice and returns a clean narration track, checks it word for word by listening back, and lays it under the video with captions.
- [Social Calendar Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-calendar-manager/): It keeps each brand's posting calendar: brand profiles, which accounts belong to which brand, the weekly posting slots, and every draft and scheduled post, including bulk uploads.
- [Cross-Platform Publisher](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/cross-platform-publisher/): It checks a post against each platform's rules, uploads its media, publishes it now or cross-posts it, and withdraws it if needed.
- [Social Analytics Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter/): It reports reach and engagement per post and per day, follower growth and tracking-link results across every connected account, X and the Facebook Page.
- [Content Calendar Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/content-calendar-producer/): Works from the posting calendar so every upcoming slot has an approved, checked image or video, starts production when one is missing and schedules the post on each network.
- [Engineering Story Writer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/engineering-story-writer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/engineering-story-writer/): Picks a real part of the product, traces how it works, writes a plain-language explainer post from that trace, and schedules it on social platforms after the voice edit.

### Speaks to people

- [Social Community Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-community-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-community-manager/): It reads the thread, replies in the brand voice, and records each reply with the post it belongs to.

### Audits and guards

- [Brand Compliance Checker](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/brand-compliance-checker) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/brand-compliance-checker/): Checks every asset before it goes out: only approved words on screen, no web addresses or slide numbers, readable contrast, and the right size and format for the channel.
