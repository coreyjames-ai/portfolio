# The Morning Brief

**For an owner who wants one message a day.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/morning-brief/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

Before the day starts the agent reads yesterday's payments, the sales pipeline, the response to every post and email, the health of the machines and the compute bill. It writes one page, records it as an audio briefing and sends both. Anything waiting on the owner's decision sits at the top.

## Function

[Running the company](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/running-the-company/): The owner's side of the business: meetings, decisions, briefings and the work that follows them.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Revenue Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/revenue-reporter/): It answers revenue questions with figures: sales by period, subscriptions, charges and disputes.
- [Social Analytics Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/social-analytics-reporter/): It reports reach and engagement per post and per day, follower growth and tracking-link results across every connected account, X and the Facebook Page.
- [Campaign Results Analyst](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/campaign-results-analyst/): It reads how each email went, which version of an A/B test won, what each campaign earned in orders, and gives a plain recommendation for the next send.

### Speaks to people

- [Morning Operations Briefer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/morning-operations-briefer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/morning-operations-briefer/): Each morning, this agent gathers the state of the knowledge base, the queues, the database load, the storage and any open alerts, and sends one short briefing.
- [Audio Briefing Producer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/audio-briefing-producer/): Turns the week's project activity into a short narrated audio briefing that people can listen to on the move.

### Challenges decisions

- [Pipeline Weekly Reviewer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/pipeline-weekly-reviewer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/pipeline-weekly-reviewer/): Each week it lists deals that have stalled and tasks that are overdue, writes a note on each, and sends the summary to the team's chat.

### Runs infrastructure

- [Compute Cost Reporter](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/compute-cost-reporter) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/compute-cost-reporter/): Reports what compute and AI usage cost, broken down by machine, by dataset, by team role and by model, with today's projection and storage costs alongside.

### Audits and guards

- [Reputation Watch Officer](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/reputation-watch-officer) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/reputation-watch-officer/): Several times a day it reads mentions, tags, reviews and live threads about the business, answers what it should, and sends anything urgent to the team's chat.
