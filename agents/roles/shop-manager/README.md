# The Shop Manager

**For an online shop owner.**

Canonical: https://coreyjames.ai/agents/roles/shop-manager/  
Author: Corey James · Published: 2026-11-11 · Kind: agent role  

## The story

The agent keeps the catalogue current and records every order. A customer who leaves a full basket gets a reminder. A customer waiting on an item hears when it is back in stock, and everyone hears when their order moves. Points are added, vouchers are issued and redeemed, and coupon drops go to the segments most likely to use them.

## Function

[Sales and customers](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/functions/sales-and-customers/): Inquiries, deals, orders, payments and support, kept moving after the first reply.

## The crew

The specialists behind this role, grouped by the kind of work they do.

### Does the work

- [Store Catalogue Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/store-catalogue-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/store-catalogue-manager/): It switches on the store features, keeps products and categories current one at a time or in bulk, and sets the display currency.
- [Order Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/order-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/order-desk/): It records orders as they happen or in bulk from another system, and lists them for finance and fulfilment.
- [Order Fulfilment Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/order-fulfilment-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/order-fulfilment-agent/): It confirms the order to the customer, adds them to the customer list, starts the welcome sequence, and records which offer and campaign produced the sale.
- [Points and Redemption Clerk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/points-and-redemption-clerk/): It credits points for purchases, takes them back on cancellations, and completes reward redemptions so balances are always right.
- [Voucher and Reward Desk](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/voucher-and-reward-desk/): It issues vouchers to members, redeems or revokes them, checks that a reward is valid, and reports how many codes are left.
- [Coupon Drop Manager](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/coupon-drop-manager/): It creates a coupon collection for a promotion, loads the codes, sends them in an email or text campaign, and tracks the orders and revenue the coupon brought in.

### Speaks to people

- [Abandoned Checkout Follow-up](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/abandoned-checkout-follow-up/): It finds checkouts that were started but never paid, looks up the customer, and sends one reminder with a fresh payment link.
- [Back-in-Stock Notifier](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/back-in-stock-notifier) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/back-in-stock-notifier/): It signs customers up for an alert on a product they want, and tells them when it returns.
- [Order Status Notifier](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/order-status-notifier) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/order-status-notifier/): It sends order updates to customers in the website chat, by text or by email, so fewer customers write in to ask.
- [Website Chat Agent](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/website-chat-agent/): It answers visitors in the website chat as a named agent, corrects or removes its own messages, and sends proactive messages such as order updates or announcements to one visitor or a batch.

### Audits and guards

- [Pricing Change Watchdog](https://github.com/coreyjames-ai/portfolio/tree/main/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog) · [page](https://coreyjames.ai/agents/specialists/pricing-change-watchdog/): It compares the change with the approved offer and alerts the owner when a price or link has drifted.
